# 🌌 SILENT GLITCH: THE SHADOW IS YOU
### *Haunted Castle Edition — Narrative & Psychological Horror Update*

A retro pseudo-3D raycaster survival-horror game built with Pygame, featuring **8 decaying haunted gothic chambers**, **Gothic castle doors**, **the Silent Glitch parallel dimension shift**, **Level 2 Sudden Blackout & Eternal Flame Lantern**, **Level 3 Crimson Key & Dual-Dimension Stalker**, and the cinematic **Final Revelation Ending**.

---

## 📖 The Story & Core Mystery: "The Shadow Is You"

You awake trapped within an ancient, fractured Gothic castle haunted by an ominous stalking silhouette. But this shadow is no ordinary ghost:
**The shadow following you is connected to your own forgotten past inside the castle.**

As you explore the 8 rooms and gather the lost crystals, the castle's reality unravels until the truth is finally revealed.

---

## 🏚️ Level Progression & Narrative Surprises

### **Level 1: Something Is Watching You**
- **4 Crystals Milestone:** As soon as you collect 4 crystals, a chilling whisper resonates through the halls:  
  *“You are not alone in this castle...”*
- **The Dimension Clue:** Shifting into the Silent Glitch void (`[SPACE]`) reveals a shadow silhouette watching from the doorways:  
  *“A shadow silhouette watches from the doorway... 'Remember who you were.'”*

### **Level 2: The Castle Is Changing**
- **Sudden Blackout:** At 20 seconds, sudden thunder crashes and pitch-black darkness engulfs the castle.
- **Low-Timer Warning:** If time drops low while in darkness, the reality decay accelerates.
- **The Ancient Truth:** Finding and restoring the **Eternal Flame Lantern** illuminates glowing script on the stone wall:  
  *“The castle is not evil... it is trapped like you.”*

### **Level 3: The Crimson Key & The Truth Behind The Shadow**
- **The Dual-Dimension Stalker:** The entity is invisible in the Real World; shift into the Silent Glitch void to spot and evade its spectral form.
- **The Sacred Crimson Key:** The exit portal is sealed by ancient wards. You must locate the glowing **Crimson Key** hidden in the Crypt before the final sanctuary unlocks!
- **The Final Escape:** Gather all 8 crystals and unlock the portal with the Crimson Key to trigger the **Final Revelation**.

## 🎬 The Final Revelation Ending Sequence

Upon escaping Level 3, the game transitions into a cinematic narrative ending sequence:

1. **The Final Awakening:**  
   *“The final crystal awakens. The castle falls silent, and the shadow stops following you.”*

   *(A subtle purple glitch flickers on screen before the final achievements).*

## 🎮 Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| **W / S** | Move Forward / Backward (subtle camera head-bob & wood footsteps) |
| **A / D** | Turn Left / Right |
| **Q / E** | Strafe Left / Right |
| **E** | Open / Close Gothic Castle Doors |
| **SPACE / SHIFT** | Trigger **Silent Glitch** Dimension Shift (Reality ↔ Glitch Void) |
| **ESC** | Return to Castle Menu / Pause |
